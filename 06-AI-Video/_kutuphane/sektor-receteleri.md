# Sektör Reçeteleri — hazır kurgu + prompt iskeletleri

> Her reçete: amaç → 4–6 çekimlik kurgu → her çekim için doldurulmuş prompt. `<…>` yerine marka brief'ten ürün/mekan kartını koy. Grade ve negatif satırını da brief'ten al. Süreler 8 sn'lik klip varsayımıyla; kurguda 3–5 sn'lik en iyi kısımlar kullanılır.

---

## A. Kozmetik / cilt bakımı — "Ürün lansmanı reels" (Nefin tipi)

**Amaç:** tek ürünü 20–30 sn'de tanıt; doku + fayda + ritüel.

| # | Çekim | Amaç |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Makro damla | hook, doku |
| 2 | Hero ürün push-in | ürün tanıma |
| 3 | İçerik metaforu (portakal / altın tanecik / su) | fayda hissi |
| 4 | El + ürün uygulama (yüz yok) | kullanım |
| 5 | Flat-lay rutin seti | çapraz satış |
| 6 | (post) logo + CTA | kapanış |

**1 — Makro damla**
```text
Cinematic skincare commercial, photorealistic. <PRODUCT SHEET>. A single translucent golden drop forms at the tip of the glass pipette, hangs for a moment, then falls and lands on pale travertine, spreading into a thin glossy ring. Extreme close-up, 100mm macro lens, shallow depth of field, locked-off camera, slow motion. Soft warm window light from the left, specular highlights on the glass and the drop. <GRADE>. Calm, precise, luxurious. No text, no logos, no hands, no face. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**2 — Hero ürün**
```text
Cinematic product hero shot, photorealistic. <PRODUCT SHEET> stands centered on cream linen draped over pale travertine, a soft-focus dried pampas stem in the background. The camera performs a slow dolly push-in from a 45-degree angle, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. Soft diffused window light with a subtle rim light outlining the bottle. <GRADE>. Still, confident, premium. No text, no logos, no people. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**3 — İçerik metaforu (C vitamini)**
```text
Editorial beauty film, photorealistic. A fresh orange slice rests on wet pale travertine beside <PRODUCT SHEET>; tiny water droplets bead on the fruit. A gentle rack focus moves from the orange in the foreground to the bottle behind it. Close-up, 100mm macro, very shallow depth of field, locked-off camera. Bright morning window light, soft caustic reflections from a nearby water surface. <GRADE>. Fresh, clean, energetic but calm. No text, no logos. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**3b — İçerik metaforu (24K altın tonik)**
```text
Extreme close-up inside the liquid: fine gold flakes drift slowly and tumble in translucent golden fluid, catching soft light as they turn. 100mm macro lens, locked-off camera, slow motion. Soft warm side light, no sparkle effects, no glitter, physically accurate suspended particles. <GRADE>. Hypnotic, luxurious, slow. No text, no logos. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**4 — El + ürün uygulama**
```text
Cinematic skincare commercial, photorealistic. A woman's hand with short natural nails (no polish) holds <PRODUCT SHEET> and squeezes the pipette; two drops fall onto the back of her other hand and she gently spreads the serum with one fingertip, the serum leaving a soft glossy sheen. Close-up on hands only, no face in frame, 50mm lens, subtle handheld movement. Soft warm window light, cream linen background out of focus. <GRADE>. Gentle, unhurried, ritual-like. Exactly five fingers on each hand. No text, no logos. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**5 — Flat-lay rutin**
```text
Top-down flat lay, photorealistic. On cream linen over pale travertine: <PRODUCT SHEET 1>, <PRODUCT SHEET 2>, <PRODUCT SHEET 3> arranged in a loose diagonal with a cotton pad and a single dried botanical stem. The camera slowly rises and drifts to the right (subtle crane up). 35mm lens, deep focus. Soft diffused daylight, gentle shadows. <GRADE>. Orderly, calm, editorial. No text, no logos, no hands. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**Kullanım varyantı — havuz kenarı (mevcut Nefin videosunun devamı):**
```text
Cinematic summer skincare commercial, photorealistic. <PRODUCT SHEET> stands on the sunlit stone edge of a turquoise pool, two fresh orange slices beside it, soft-focus white sun loungers in the background. Caustic light patterns from the water ripple across the bottle. The camera performs a slow lateral tracking move to the right. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. Bright late-morning sun with a soft fill, no harsh blown highlights. <GRADE> with a touch of warm turquoise in the water. Fresh, summery, calm. No text, no logos, no people. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

## B. Klinik / psikoloji merkezi — "Sakin mekan B-roll" (Humentis tipi)

**Amaç:** gerçek çekilen terapist videosunun arasına giren, mekan/ışık/sükûnet hissi veren 5–6 klip. İnsan yok.

| # | Çekim | Amaç |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kapı açılışı, içeri giren ışık | "kapımız açık" metaforu |
| 2 | Boş danışma odası, koltuk | güven, sakinlik |
| 3 | Pencereden gelen ışık, keten perde | nefes |
| 4 | Koridor ve kapı numaraları (14 oda) | ölçek |
| 5 | Bitki / yaprak makro | marka botanik dili |
| 6 | Bekleme alanı | erişilebilirlik |

**1 — Kapı açılışı**
```text
Quiet documentary-style interior film, photorealistic. A pale oak door with a small brass number plate opens slowly inward, and soft daylight spills across a cream corridor floor. No people, the door moves as if opened gently from inside. Medium shot, 35mm lens, locked-off camera, real-time speed. Soft overcast daylight, gentle volumetric light through the doorway, no dramatic shadows. <GRADE>. Calm, welcoming, unhurried. No text, no logos, no signage text. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**2 — Boş danışma odası**
```text
Quiet interior film, photorealistic. <LOCATION SHEET>. The camera glides forward very slowly on a gimbal toward the empty armchair. 35mm lens, moderate depth of field. Soft daylight through linen curtains, warm-neutral tone, no lamps on. <GRADE>. Safe, calm, private. No people, no text, no logos, no medical equipment. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**3 — Pencere ışığı**
```text
Close-up of a linen curtain gently moving in a slight breeze, soft daylight diffusing through the fabric, a blurred sage-green armchair behind it. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, locked-off camera, slow motion. <GRADE>. Breathing, peaceful. No people, no text. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**4 — Koridor**
```text
Quiet interior film, photorealistic. A long cream corridor with a row of pale oak doors, each with a small brass number plate (numbers blurred/illegible), a single plant at the far end. Slow tracking shot moving forward along the corridor at walking pace. 24mm lens, deep focus. Even soft daylight from side windows. <GRADE>. Orderly, calm, spacious. No people, no text, no logos. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**5 — Botanik makro**
```text
Extreme close-up of soft green leaves of an indoor plant, light moving slowly across them as a cloud passes, tiny details of leaf veins. 100mm macro, shallow depth of field, locked-off camera. Soft daylight. <GRADE>. Living, gentle, still. No text. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**Kural:** Hastane/medikal hiçbir obje yok; danışan yok; çocuk yok. Terapist konuşması gerçek çekim; bu klipler onun üzerine/arasına girer.

## C. Kurumsal / yazılım — "Ürün tanıtım" (Hemshare tipi)

**Amaç:** uygulamayı gerçek hayatta göster; ekran içeriği post'ta bindirilir.

| # | Çekim | Amaç |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dernek toplantı masası, çay, evrak kaosu | problem |
| 2 | Telefon elde, yeşil ekran | çözüm (UI post'ta) |
| 3 | Laptop masada, yeşil ekran | yönetici paneli |
| 4 | Topluluk geniş plan (uzak, yüz detayı yok) | "üye ağı" |
| 5 | (post) logo + CTA | kapanış |

**1 — Problem**
```text
Documentary-style corporate film, photorealistic. A wooden meeting table in a modest association office: stacks of paper folders, a printed member list, two glasses of Turkish tea, a landline phone. Slow top-down push-in. 35mm lens, deep focus. Overcast daylight from a window, natural colors. <GRADE>. Honest, slightly cluttered, real. No people, no readable text, no logos. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

**2 — Telefon (yeşil ekran)**
```text
Corporate product film, photorealistic. <PRODUCT SHEET: phone with solid green screen>. A man's hand in a navy sweater sleeve holds the phone at a slight angle; his thumb taps the screen once. Over-the-shoulder close-up, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, subtle handheld. Soft daylight from a window. The screen is a perfectly flat, evenly lit solid green (#00FF00) with no reflections, no content, no UI. <GRADE>. Calm, competent. No text, no logos. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

**4 — Topluluk**
```text
Documentary-style film, photorealistic. A wide shot of a multi-generational group of about eight people seated around a long table in a bright association hall, seen from the back of the room, faces small and not in focus, some raising hands, tea glasses on the table. Slow crane up. 24mm lens, deep focus. Soft overcast daylight. <GRADE>. Warm, communal, real. No readable text, no logos, no exaggerated smiles, natural body language. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

## D. Logo animasyonu (her sektör)

AI video modelleri logoyu bozar. Tercih sırası:
1. **After Effects / Apple Motion / Canva / Keynote (Magic Move)** ile vektör logodan animasyon — Humentis'teki gibi (`humentis-logo-animasyonu.mp4` bu yöntemle yapıldı: 1080×1080, 6 sn).
2. Zorunluysa image-to-video, minimum hareket:
```text
The logo image stays perfectly still and unchanged. A soft warm light sweep passes slowly from left to right across the surface once. Very subtle. No morphing, no added elements, no text change.
```
Sonucu kare kare kontrol et; en küçük bozulmada 1. yönteme dön.

## E. Sunum / web hero arka planı (her sektör)

Metinsiz, yavaş, döngüye girebilen "ambient" klip; üzerine site başlığı biner.
```text
Abstract but photorealistic slow-motion macro of cream silk fabric gently rippling, soft warm light, very shallow depth of field, seamless slow movement suitable for looping, no text, no objects. <GRADE>. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```
Marka rengine göre malzeme değiştir: Nefin → silk/linen; Humentis → linen curtain + leaf shadows; Hemshare → navy paper texture with soft light.
